Strip — the webtoon sketchpad

Draw, ink and lay out your comics and webtoons anywhere. When you're ready, post your story online so readers can find it, follow it, vote and comment.

Open StripPrivacy Policy

What you can do with Strip

Draw

Brushes, an eraser, a blend tool, selection tools, layers, symmetry, fill, a colour wheel and importable .abr brushes.

Make stories

Organise your work into stories and chapters, then lay out long vertical-scroll pages in the webtoon style.

Publish & read

Post a story online, browse what others have made, follow your favourites, vote and leave comments. Reading never needs an account.

Your work stays yours

Drawings are stored on your own device by default. Only stories you choose to post are uploaded.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional and only needed to post stories, comment, vote or follow. Strip uses Google Sign-In and only receives your basic Google profile: your name, profile picture, and email address. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google data.

Strip's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See our Privacy Policy for details.
