Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Strip app and website (“Strip”, “we”, “us”) collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Strip you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Strip is a webtoon and comic sketchpad with an optional online community for posting and reading stories. Contact: stripcomics412@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

Stored on your device

Your drawings, stories, chapters, settings and preferences are saved in your browser's local storage (such as IndexedDB and localStorage) on your own device. We cannot see this data and it is not uploaded unless you choose to post a story. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Google Sign-In (optional)

If you sign in with Google, Google shares with us your name, profile picture, email address and a unique Google account identifier. We do not request any other Google scopes and never access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar or other Google services.

Content you post

If you post a story online, we store the story's details (title, genre, synopsis, status, thumbnail), its chapters and page images, your public profile (display name, bio, avatar), and your comments, votes, follows and notifications. Posted stories, your profile name, bio, avatar and comments are public.

Usage and technical data

Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) and the services we rely on may process technical data such as IP address, browser type and request logs to deliver the service and keep it secure. We keep simple view counts for posted stories.

3. How we use information

  • To sign you in and to let you post, edit and delete your own stories, comments, votes and follows.
  • To show your public profile and content to other readers.
  • To send in-app notifications (for example, new chapters of stories you follow).
  • To moderate content, prevent spam and abuse, and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To keep Strip secure, working and improving.

We use your Google email address only to identify your account and for moderation and security. It is not shown publicly, sold, or used for advertising.

4. Google user data and Limited Use

Strip's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular, we only use Google user data to provide and improve the user-facing features described above; we do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide the service, comply with law, or with your consent; we do not use it for advertising, including retargeting or interest-based ads; and no humans read it except with your consent, for security or abuse investigations, or to comply with law.

5. Advertising and third parties

Strip may show ads served by third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense and other ad providers. These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads and may collect information such as your IP address and device details. Google's use of advertising cookies is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads, and you can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com. We do not give advertisers your Google profile information.

Other services we use to run Strip: Google Identity Services (sign-in), Cloudflare (hosting and storage) and Google Fonts (fonts). Each has its own privacy policy.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run Strip (such as Cloudflare), when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the service.

7. Cookies and local storage

Strip uses local storage and caching to save your work, remember settings and make the app load faster and work offline. Ad providers may set their own cookies. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

8. Retention and deletion

We keep posted content and account data until you delete it or ask us to. You can take a story offline from within the app, which removes its chapters and images. To delete your profile, comments and other data, email stripcomics412@gmail.com from the address on your Google account and we will remove it within a reasonable time. You can also revoke Strip's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

9. Security

Sign-in tokens are verified by our server against Google's public keys, and only the owner of a story can change or delete it. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

Strip is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it. Where the law of your country sets a higher minimum age, you must meet it to sign in.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact us at the address above to make a request.

12. International users

Strip is hosted on global infrastructure, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own.

13. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

14. Contact

Questions or requests: stripcomics412@gmail.com.
